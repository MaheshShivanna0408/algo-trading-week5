## Questions to practise answering

- Why does a general language model need extra training on financial text?
- What is the difference between pre-training and fine-tuning?
- What does "attention" let the model do that older models (RNNs/LSTMs) found difficult?
- How are the two papers connected? (FinBERT is built on BERT, which uses the Transformer encoder from Paper 2.)
- Our week 5 notebook tests FinBERT on Financial PhraseBank. Why might its score there be higher than on our news headlines?
- If FinBERT labels a headline wrongly, how could that affect a trading decision?

**Rule for the practice:** everyone speaks. If someone gets stuck, the group helps them find the answer in the paper rather than answering for them.
